# 🚀 Day 99 – Arrays & Greedy

Today I practiced two problems involving array optimization and custom sorting.

## 📌 Problems Solved

1. Maximum Product of Three Numbers
2. Largest Number

---

### 1. Maximum Product of Three Numbers
Tracked the three largest and two smallest values in a single traversal. The maximum product can come either from the three largest numbers or from the largest number combined with the two smallest negative numbers.

### 2. Largest Number
Converted the numbers into an Integer array and used a custom comparator. Two numbers `a` and `b` are ordered based on whether `ab` or `ba` produces the larger concatenated value.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Arrays
- Greedy Thinking
- Single-Pass Traversal
- Custom Comparator
- Sorting
- Negative Numbers
- Optimization

---

## 📊 Complexity

### Maximum Product of Three Numbers
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Largest Number
- Time: O(n log n)
- Space: O(n)

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway

Today's problems reinforced that identifying the right candidates or comparison rule can eliminate unnecessary brute force.
